Rights Metrics

The rights pillar evaluates several core metrics:

- genderEqualityIndex — Measures access, opportunity, and safety across
  gender identities.
- identityInclusionScore — Tracks protections and inclusion for diverse
  identities.
- bodilyAutonomyAccessIndex — Measures access to essential healthcare
  and personal autonomy.
- antiDiscriminationSignal — Flags systemic patterns of exclusion.
- violenceRiskIndex — Tracks exposure to harm or instability.
- rightsProtectionIndex — Measures how well rights are upheld across
  systems.

These metrics are neutral and descriptive. They help identify areas where
rights are strong and where additional support may be needed.
