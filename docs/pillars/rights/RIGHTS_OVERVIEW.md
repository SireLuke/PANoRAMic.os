Universal Human Rights Pillar

The Universal Human Rights Pillar tracks dignity, safety, access, and
non-discrimination across all populations. It does not enforce policy or
advocate for specific political outcomes. Instead, it provides neutral,
structured metrics that help communities understand where rights are strong
and where they are under strain.

This pillar integrates with ecological, cultural, labor, commons, and
market systems to ensure that human well-being is represented across the
entire OS.
