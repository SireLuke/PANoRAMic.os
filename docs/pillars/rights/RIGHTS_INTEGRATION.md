Rights Integration

The rights pillar connects to multiple parts of PANoRAMic.os:

- worldState — Stores rights metrics and updates them as signals change.
- globalSignals — Emits rights-related signals for other pillars.
- RAMS audits — Evaluates consistency, accountability, and data quality.
- dashboard — Displays rights scores and trends.
- CLI — Provides commands for inspecting rights metrics.
- PAR economy — Ensures dignity baselines and access metrics align with
  economic metabolism.

This integration ensures that human rights remain visible across ecological,
cultural, labor, commons, and market systems.
