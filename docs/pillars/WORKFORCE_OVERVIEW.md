Eco-Humanitarian Workforce System

The Eco-Humanitarian Workforce System provides purpose-driven employment for
individuals who want to contribute to ecological restoration, community repair,
and humanitarian support. It offers PAR compensation for work that improves
planetary stability and human well-being.

This system supports unemployed individuals, disenfranchised communities,
unhoused populations, and people transitioning into new roles. It is designed
to restore dignity, build skills, and strengthen cooperative infrastructure.
