Abstract
PANoRAMic.os is an open‑source planetary coordination engine designed to stabilize ecological systems, infrastructure networks, markets, and human dignity through continuous, real‑time global auditing and adaptive resource management.

The system integrates ecological data, infrastructure signals, market flows, humanitarian metrics, and planetary risk indicators into a unified operating system capable of adjusting resource velocity, extraction pressure, stewardship compensation, and dignity floors.

PANoRAMic.os functions as a planetary nervous system, planetary immune system, and planetary economic circulatory system, providing continuous stabilization and regeneration across all major planetary domains.

2. System Architecture Overview
PANoRAMic.os consists of four primary layers:

2.1 RAMS — Resource Audit & Management System
RAMS is the diagnostic layer.
It continuously evaluates:

ecological regeneration

infrastructure load

energy stability

food/water security

market cooperation

humanitarian conditions

node network health

catastrophe probability

RAMS produces a global stability score every tick.

2.2 PAR — Panitarian Asset Receipt Economy
PAR is the circulatory layer.
It is a dignity‑first, non‑extractive planetary resource flow issued to humans based on planetary conditions.

PAR adjusts:

mint rate

velocity

dignity floors

stewardship salaries

extraction pressure

node evolution rewards

⭐ Soft‑Pegged PAR Supply Cap
PAR supply is capped by a soft‑peg tied to humanity and planetary resources:

𝑃𝐴𝑅 max=(Global Population×1.35)×𝑅𝑆𝐷𝑉
Where:

RSDV — Resource Scarcity Dynamic Vector
RSDV is a multiplier between 0.65 and 1.00 that adjusts PAR supply based on real‑time planetary resource conditions.

RSDV is computed from RAMS audits of:

water scarcity

food production stability

energy availability

ecological regeneration rate

mineral extraction pressure

recycling throughput

biome health

climate volatility

RSDV Behavior
RSDV = 1.00 → resources abundant → full PAR soft‑peg

RSDV ≈ 0.85 → moderate scarcity → PAR tightens

RSDV ≈ 0.65 → severe scarcity → PAR contracts sharply

RSDV ensures PAR supply reflects real planetary limits, preventing inflation, collapse, or runaway extraction.

2.3 Demurrage System — Anti‑Hoarding Mechanism
Demurrage ensures PAR continues circulating by applying dynamic decay to idle or hoarded resources.

Demurrage prevents:

dead zones

resource bottlenecks

speculative accumulation

market stagnation

It keeps PAR behaving like blood, not gold.

2.4 Stagnation Metric — Dead Zone Detection
The Stagnation Metric identifies when parts of the planetary system stop:

regenerating

circulating

contributing

stabilizing

When stagnation rises, PAN triggers:

demurrage adjustments

stewardship boosts

dignity floor reinforcement

extraction pressure reduction

node evolution incentives

immune system activation
