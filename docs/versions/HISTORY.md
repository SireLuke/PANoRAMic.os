v0.4 — Infrastructure Concepts

v0.5 — Modes and Micro-AI Planning
- Designed Light Mode and Full Planetary Mode.
- Added micro-AI retrieval and cultural nuance layer to roadmap.
- Planned cooperative knowledge mesh and future language scaffolding.
