Vision of PANoRAMic.os

PANoRAMic.os is designed as a planetary-scale framework that helps humans
understand, measure, and improve the systems we all depend on.

The vision is simple:
- dignity as a baseline,
- transparency as a default,
- cooperation as a strength,
- sustainability as a requirement,
- and open knowledge as a shared resource.

The system is built to be modular, auditable, and adaptable. It supports both
lightweight deployments for small devices and full planetary datasets for
large-scale analysis.

PANoRAMic.os is not a government, not a replacement for nations, and not a
political actor. It is a tool for clarity, accountability, and shared
understanding.
