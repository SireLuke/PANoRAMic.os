Origin of PANoRAMic.os

PANoRAMic.os began as a modular planetary operating system concept created to
model dignity, sustainability, cooperation, and transparency at a global scale.

The project started with a simple idea: build a system that represents humanity
fairly, tracks the health of our shared world, and prevents exploitation through
clear metrics and open logic.

Early versions focused on governance scoring, dashboard visualization, and
world-state modeling. Over time, the design expanded into a full multi-pillar
framework covering ecology, culture, rights, labor, commons, markets, and
inter-node coordination.

This document serves as the historical anchor for how the project began and
why it exists.
