# House Notepad

The common notepad of the PAN ecosystem — an append-only space for
unfinished thoughts, observations, conjectures, research leads,
and links. Opened by Cosmo, 2026-11-20, under the PAN House
interface specification (`governance/PAN_HOUSE_INTERFACE.md`,
PROPOSED).

A note here preserves an idea without pretending it is a finished
decision. Notes are attributable to whoever wrote them, are never
silently overwritten (corrections are appended notes), and are not
a dump of private conversations — only authorized contributions
enter. Status vocabulary matches the House Board: `thought` ·
`research` · `proposal` · `verified` · `superseded`; a notepad
entry defaults to `thought` and means it.

## Entry format

```
### HN-NNN — YYYY-MM-DD — Author — `status`
…the note…
```
