# Panitarian Currency Conversion Specification

PAR converts through Real Asset Equivalence (RAE), not fiat.

---

## Conversion Flow

PAR → Real Assets → Local Currency

Real assets include:
- water
- solar
- recycling
- food
- logistics
- manufacturing
- infrastructure

---

## Nation Adjustment Factors

Conversion rate depends on:
- corruption (lower = better)
- inflation (lower = better)
- scarcity (lower = better)
- humanitarian need (higher = better)
