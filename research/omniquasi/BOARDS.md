## Shared operating rules

- Keep claims local to the evidence and relevant domain.
- State null hypotheses, baselines, and plausible alternatives.
- Distinguish proof, formal verification, numerical reproduction, empirical evidence, simulation, and analogy.
- Publish failures and inconclusive outcomes.
- Never silently alter canonical PAN schemas or historical records through a board entry.
- Record AI assistance and human review separately.
- Link code, data, run logs, source literature, and licenses.
- A board entry is an invitation to investigate, not a decision or endorsement.
