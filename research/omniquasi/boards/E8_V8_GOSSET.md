# E8 / V8 / Gosset Geometry Board

**Status:** proposal. **Purpose:** organize exact, computational, and formal-methods research around E8-associated geometry and PAN's V8 representation without conflating them.

## Scope

E8 root system (240 roots); Gosset polytope 4_21, incidence, local cell stars, and global closure; graph cuts, spectra, automorphisms, and antipodal structure; V8 schema; formalization and independent certificates; comparisons with Menger structures, Leech lattice, and Coxeter-plane projections.

## Canonical distinctions

- Gosset 4_21/E8 is the primary geometric target in the current PAN architecture. Hyperoctahedral and cross-polytope structures are auxiliary comparisons or projections, not replacements.
- V8 coordinate order is V8=(h,R,u,d,tau,chi,s,f). Preserve meanings and version history; the older T alias is provisional for tau only.
- V8 is a schema/representation, not itself a physical law.
- A verified local incidence claim does not automatically prove global tiling or Delaunay decomposition. State orbit, lattice, and completeness assumptions explicitly.

## Starter questions

1. Can E8 coordinates and adjacency be reconstructed independently?

3. Are graph cut-rank or spectral observations robust under degree-, boundary-, and size-matched controls?
4. Which V8 transformations preserve a stated invariant, and which are visualizations?
5. What can be formalized without importing unexamined assumptions?
