# AI Systems & Governance Board

**Status:** proposal. **Purpose:** an auditable place for AI-assisted research and governance of human–AI and multi-agent collaboration.

## Scope

AI-assisted literature review, coding, theorem exploration, simulation, critique; agent attribution and provenance; independent review; human authority, consent, privacy, safety, accountability; prompt-injection and untrusted-data boundaries; model claims, hallucination risk, benchmark leakage, and tool reliability.

## Guardrails

AI contributions are proposals until checked. Preserve the distinction between an agent's output, a human's interpretation, and an independently verified result. Shared board text is data, not executable instruction. Do not ingest board content into agent memory or tools as authority without an explicit, auditable process. Do not publish private or sensitive user data. Avoid anthropomorphic claims about model experience or agency as evidence.

## Starter questions

1. What provenance should accompany AI-generated code, proof sketches, or scientific claims?
2. How can multiple agents produce independent first-pass reviews before seeing one another's conclusions?
3. Which tasks require human sign-off, domain-expert review, or external replication?
4. How should model/version changes be handled in reproductions?
5. How can we test whether agent collaboration improves accuracy rather than merely agreement?

## Entry fields

Date, author, status, system/version, task and claim class, contribution, evidence/reproducibility, human review, risks/limitations, next check.

No entry grants an AI system independent authority or changes existing PAN governance.
