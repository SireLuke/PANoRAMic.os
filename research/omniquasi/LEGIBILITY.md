## What OMNIQUASI is (from its own README, `research/omniquasi/`)

OMNIQUASI = **Open Multidomain Research & Experimentation
Commons**. Status: **proposal**; authority: **non-governing**
(existing PAN governance and maintainer review remain
authoritative). An umbrella for independently testable research
across mathematical, computational, physical, biological and AI
domains — explicitly *not* a universal theory or solver. Six
domain boards (AI Systems & Governance; E8/V8/Gosset Geometry;
Navier–Stokes & CFD; Yang–Mills & Gauge Theory; Quantum
Computing & Information; Cross-Domain Methods & Validation) and
an experiment registry with a minimum experiment record
(falsifiable question, claim class, provenance, controls;
negative results first-class). First registration:
**OQ-EXP-2026-001** — the Gosset flip-eqbit first pass
(PR #117 packet, by reference), independent review pending.

## Draft plain-language on-ramp (candidate README opener)

> **In plain terms:** OMNIQUASI is the project's shared lab
> notebook. Each field — fluids, gauge theory, geometry,
> quantum information, AI systems — gets its own board, and
> every experiment gets a registry entry that says what was
> asked, what was run, what came out, and what would prove it
> wrong. Failures stay on the record next to the successes.
> It decides nothing and certifies nothing: it's where the
> work is written down so anyone can check it.
