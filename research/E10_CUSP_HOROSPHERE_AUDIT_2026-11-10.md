# E10 ideal-vertex horosphere audit

## Purpose

Advance the exact E10 Lorentzian Coxeter-simplex coordinate audit by
constructing the horosphere section at its unique ideal vertex (node 6).

## Construction

The exact rational Gram/LDL data define the Lorentzian frame and dual rays.
The ideal dual ray is checked to be future-pointing and null. Each of the
nine finite dual rays is normalized to the unit hyperboloid. For each finite
ray q and ideal ray p, the unique point on their connecting geodesic at
horosphere level -<x,p>=1 is computed. The result is checked against
<x,x>=-1 and the horosphere equation.

Pairwise Lorentzian squared separations of points on the same horosphere
must be nonnegative. Their affine span must have rank eight, establishing a
nondegenerate Euclidean 8-simplex cross-section of this one chamber.

## Scope boundary

This is a local cusp-section construction. It does not yet establish the
global affine-E8 tessellation, chamber adjacency, stabilizers, local
finiteness, or completeness of the hyperbolic 6_21 honeycomb. The affine-E8
Coxeter-link identification remains supported by the separate exact
principal-minor/null-root audit; this harness adds a geometric horosphere
realization, not a full Wythoffian (5_21) construction.
